# Prayer & Pulpit Formulas — Mark, Church of the Crossroads

Built from nine sermons (2019–2026): The Cure to Church Drama, Indivisible, A People of Purpose, Algorithms and Agendas, People Matter More, To Know God, Peace Love and Victory, the Anthony & Evian wedding, and Mejor Perdón (your own Spanish). Every formula is your phrasing, moved into NBLA-register Northern Mexican Spanish. Gloss in English on the line below.

Register rules applied throughout: **orar** (never rezar), **el culto / el servicio** (never la misa), **pastor** (never padre), **la Santa Cena** (never eucaristía), **la Palabra**, **la Escritura**, **iglesia** as a vocative, **ustedes** to the congregation, **tú** to God.

Marked **[FLAG]** = English idiom with no clean equivalent; the Spanish given is a replacement, not a translation. **[verify NBLA]** = Scripture wording from memory; check against your NBLA before preaching.

## 7. Confession / consecration prayers (your written ones)

### The Cure to Church Drama — closing prayer
**Jesús, Tú prometiste que donde dos o más se reúnen en Tu nombre, Tú estás presente.**
Jesus, You promised that where two or more gather in Your name, You are present.
**Así que aquí estoy, aquí estamos, expuestos delante de Ti.**
So here I am — here we are — standing exposed before You.
**Confieso que le he echado leña al conflicto,** *(idiom: echar leña al fuego)*
I confess that I've added fuel to conflict,
**que he hablado cuando debí escuchar,**
that I've talked when I should have listened,
**que he juzgado cuando debí mostrar misericordia.**
that I've judged when I should have shown mercy.
**Confieso que he elegido defender mi orgullo en lugar de proteger la unidad.**
I confess that I've chosen to defend my pride instead of protecting unity.
**Confieso que he dejado que la amargura se asiente en mi corazón, y la he cargado como un arma.**
I confess that I've let bitterness sit in my heart, and I've carried it like a weapon.
**Confieso que he visto a otros como el problema, mientras ignoro el desastre que hay en mí.**
I confess that I've looked at others as the problem, while ignoring the mess in me.
**Pero Tú no le tienes miedo a mi drama.**
But You are not afraid of my drama.
**No te sorprenden mi enojo, mis celos, mi falta de perdón.**
You are not shocked by my anger, my jealousy, my unforgiveness.
**Tú entras en él. Tú lo redimes.**
You step into it. You redeem it.
**Así que juntos decimos: Jesús, te invito A mi drama más desastroso.**
So together we say: Jesus, I invite You INTO my messiest drama.
**Te entrego las partes de mí que causan división.**
I give You the parts of me that stir division.
**Rindo mi necesidad de tener la razón.**
I surrender my need to be right.
**Abro mi corazón para que Tú sanes, reconcilies y restaures. Amén.**
I open my heart for You to heal, to reconcile, to restore. Amen.

## 14. Idioms flagged as having no clean Spanish equivalent

These recur in your preaching. Don't calque them; use the replacement or drop the image.

- **"I get it"** → *lo entiendo* / *ya sé* / *te entiendo*. Never *lo agarro*.
- **"Get over yourself / get over ourselves"** → *dejar el ego a un lado*, *quitarnos del centro*. *Superarse* means self-improvement.
- **"Chew on that"** → *rumiar* (Bible-meditation word) or *piénsenlo*.
- **"Marching orders"** → *instrucciones*, *tarea*, *encargo*.
- **"Smack dab in the middle"** → *justo en medio*.
- **"Don't take it from me"** → *no me lo crean a mí*, *no lo digo yo*.
- **"Go through the motions"** → *hacerlo por inercia*, *pasar por la rutina*.
- **"Iron sharpening iron"** → this one DOES exist: *el hierro con hierro se afila* (Prov 27:17 NBLA — verify).
- **"Church drama / church hurt"** → *drama* is used in Mexican Spanish, but *las heridas de iglesia* for "church hurt".
- **"Speak up"** → *di lo que piensas*, *exprésate*; *habla* alone loses the "up".
- **"Sock me square on the jaw"** → *me dio un puñetazo en la quijada*; *me dio un buen golpe* if you want the register.
- **"Treat church like a buffet"** → *tratar la iglesia como un bufet* works as-is in Mexico.
- **"Fill our spiritual bellies"** → *llenar la panza espiritual* is fine and funny; keep it.
- **"Hot Topic"** (the store) → keep the brand; *no parecía una tienda de Hot Topic*.
- **"Con-sumers / Christ followers"** wordplay → does not survive; use *seguidores de Cristo, no consumidores* and let the contrast carry it.
- **"Tinfoil on / tinfoil off"** → *gorro de aluminio puesto / gorro de aluminio fuera* is understood by younger Mexicans; older ones won't get it.
- **"My heart for this message is..."** → *mi anhelo con este mensaje es...* / *mi deseo*. *Mi corazón para* is a calque.
- **"Made a way"** → *abrió camino*.
- **"Lean into"** (loyalty, dedication) → *apoyarse en* / *entregarse a*; no direct match.
- **"Rewind the Spirit"** → *regresarle al Espíritu* (Mex); the joke lands.
